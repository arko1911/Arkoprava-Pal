## AInsurCo — Strategy & Analytics Consultant · London (remote) · Dec 2024 – Mar 2025 and Sep 2025 – Jan 2026

An actuarial and insurance-tech consultancy, across two engagements. My work was checking that numbers were right before anyone made decisions with them.

### First engagement · Dec 2024 – Mar 2025

**Reconciling pension statements.** Rebuilt annual benefit statements for **74 Irish pension test cases** from system tables, line by line: transfers in, growth, charges and value share, plus recalculated management charges, fund-switching rules and growth rates.

**IFRS 17 reporting in Power BI.** Built my own reporting dashboards for the new insurance accounting standard: P&L, reconciliations of the key reserve components, the transition from the old standard, validation checks, and what-if shocks (e.g. lapse rates 3% vs 10%).

### Second engagement · Sep 2025 – Jan 2026

**Validating a pricing model.** The firm had rebuilt its life-insurance pricing in Python. I validated it (I didn't build it) against the legacy Excel/VBA models and expected outputs across product lines:

| Product line | Cases | Matched |
|---|---|---|
| Life & specified illness | 2,797 | 2,716 (97.1%) |
| Term assurance | 716 | 611 |
| Mortgage protection | 38 | 31 |

For the largest set, **every one of the 81 mismatches was traced and explained**, which is what turns "97% match" into a model people can trust.
